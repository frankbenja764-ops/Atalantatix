For event organisers

Sell through AtlantaTix

AtlantaTix is an independent ticket sales partner for approved sports events in Eldoret and Uasin Gishu. Organisers remain responsible for the event; AtlantaTix lists and sells the authorised ticket allocation under an agreed written arrangement.

01 / SUBMIT

Share event details

Provide organiser contacts, event date, venue, ticket categories, prices and available ticket allocation.

02 / VERIFY

Confirm authorisation

AtlantaTix checks the organiser's identity, event details and written permission to sell tickets before publishing.

03 / SETTLE

Agree payments

Before sales begin, agree in writing on fees or commission, payment collection, reporting, settlement timing, refunds and cancellations.

ATLANTATIX COMMISSION

KSh 1,000 per ticket sold

Proposed standard agent commission: AtlantaTix retains KSh 1,000 from each successfully paid ticket, and the balance is payable to the organiser under the signed agreement. Confirm the ticket price, any applicable fees, refunds and settlement process with each organiser before sales open.

Trust and safety: AtlantaTix must not advertise an event as confirmed or accept customer money until the organiser and ticket allocation are verified and the payment process is live. Never request or store a customer's M-Pesa PIN.
